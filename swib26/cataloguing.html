Learning entity-oriented metadata with EntEdit

Activity: cataloguing a publication

Each participant takes one of the thirty publications below, so that everyone can work in the same repository at the same time: no two of them share an author or a work. From the description and the catalogue record, create the work, the expression and the manifestation, and the person who wrote it, and relate them to each other. Record the publisher as text on the manifestation only. Then look at what the others have made, in the entity lists and in the search.
