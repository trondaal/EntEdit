Exercises: Works built on works (Little Women)

Catalogue each publication below. For each one, decide whether it publishes Louisa May Alcott's novel (in the original, revised, translated, shortened or read aloud), or whether it is something new based on the novel, and how. Some publications contain more than one thing. When you are done, compare your solution with the example in the database.

  1. Little Women : or, Meg, Jo, Beth and Amy / by Louisa M. Alcott ; illustrated by May Alcott. — Boston : Roberts Brothers, 1868. — 341 p. : ill.

    Content: First edition of the first volume of Alcott's novel about the four March sisters, Meg, Jo, Beth and Amy. Illustrated by the author's sister May Alcott.

  2. Little Women : or, Meg, Jo, Beth and Amy. Part second / by Louisa M. Alcott. — Boston : Roberts Brothers, 1869. — 359 p.

    Content: The second volume of the novel, continuing the story of the March sisters. In Britain this volume was published as Good Wives.

  3. Little Women : or, Meg, Jo, Beth and Amy / by Louisa M. Alcott ; illustrated by Frank T. Merrill. — New edition. — Boston : Roberts Brothers, 1880

    Content: Both volumes of the novel in one book. The text was revised for this edition: spelling, grammar and colloquial language were made more conventional. New illustrations by Frank T. Merrill.

  4. Little Men : life at Plumfield with Jo's boys / by Louisa M. Alcott. — Boston : Roberts Brothers, 1871

    Content: Alcott's follow-up to Little Women: Jo and her husband run a school for boys at Plumfield.

  5. Jo's Boys : and how they turned out : a sequel to "Little Men" / by Louisa M. Alcott. — Boston : Roberts Brothers, 1886

    Content: The last book about the March family, continuing the story of Little Men.

  6. The Annotated Little Women / Louisa May Alcott ; edited with an introduction and notes by John Matteson. — New York : W. W. Norton & Company, 2016. — lxxvii, 652 pages : illustrations ; 27 cm. — ISBN 9780393072198

    Content: The complete text of Little Women with an introduction, extensive notes and commentary by John Matteson, and many illustrations.

  7. Little Women / Louisa M. Alcott ; retold by Anne Collins. — Ismaning : Hueber, 2008. — 64 pages. — (Macmillan Guided Readers. Beginner level). — ISBN 9783193929563

    Content: A short, simplified retelling of Little Women for beginning learners of English, by Anne Collins.

  8. Little Women : original + ungekürzt / by Louisa May Alcott ; read by Laural Merlington. — Berlin : Bertz + Fischer, 2009. — 2 CDs (MP3, 18 hrs 28 min). — (Bertz + Fischer Audiobooks). — ISBN 9783865055613

    Content: Audiobook on two MP3 CDs: the complete novel in English, read by Laural Merlington. The full text of the novel is included on the discs as a PDF file.

  9. Meg, Jo, Beth, and Amy : a graphic novel : a modern retelling of Little Women / Rey Terciero ; illustrated by Bre Indigo. — New York : Little, Brown and Company, 2019. — 256 pages : color illustrations. — ISBN 9780316522885

    Content: A graphic novel that retells Little Women as a modern story set in present-day New York. Written by Rey Terciero and drawn by Bre Indigo.

  10. Little Vampire Women / Louisa May Alcott and Lynn Messina. — New York : HarperTeen, 2010. — 317 pages ; 21 cm. — ISBN 9780061976254

    Content: Alcott's story rewritten as a comic horror novel in which the March sisters are vampires.

  11. March : a novel / Geraldine Brooks. — New York : Viking, 2005. — 280 pages. — ISBN 0670033359

    Content: A novel about Mr March, the father of the March sisters, and what happens to him in the American Civil War while he is away from his family. Pulitzer Prize for Fiction 2006.

  12. Little Women : opera in two acts : vocal score / music and libretto by Mark Adamo. — New York : G. Schirmer, [s.a.]. — ISBN 9780634020926

    Content: Vocal score of Mark Adamo's opera based on Little Women, first performed by Houston Grand Opera in 1998. Adamo wrote both the music and the libretto.

  13. Les quatre filles du docteur March / L.M. Alcott ; traduction de P.J. Stahl. — Genève : Famot, 1974. — 323 p., [12] f. : ill. ; 19 cm

    Content: French version of Little Women by P.-J. Stahl, the pen name of the publisher Pierre-Jules Hetzel, first published in 1880. A free version that shortens and changes the text.

  14. Les quatre filles du docteur March / Louisa May Alcott ; trad. nouv. d'Anne Joba ; [dossier de Nicole Bon]. — Paris : France loisirs, 1992. — 285 p. ; 19 cm. — (Collection Jeunes). — ISBN 2-7242-6927-6

    Content: French translation by Anne Joba, with a dossier by Nicole Bon.

  15. Les quatre filles du docteur March / Louisa May Alcott ; traduit de l'anglais par Paulette Vielhomme-Callais ; illustrations de Rozier-Gaudriault. — Paris : Gallimard, 2009. — 1 vol. (374 p.) : ill. + 1 DVD vidéo (1 h 54 min) ; 18 cm. — (Folio junior ; 413. Folio junior cinéma). — ISBN 978-2-07-062851-3

    Content: French translation by Paulette Vielhomme-Callais, sold together with a DVD of the 1994 film of Little Women, directed by Gillian Armstrong, with Winona Ryder as Jo.

  16. Les quatre filles du docteur March / Louisa May Alcott ; traduit et abrégé par Malika Ferdjoukh. — Texte abrégé. — Paris : L'École des loisirs, 2019. — 234 p. ; 19 cm. — (Classiques). — ISBN 978-2-211-30465-8

    Content: French translation by Malika Ferdjoukh, who also shortened the text.

  17. Little Women : Beth und ihre Schwestern / Louisa May Alcott ; aus dem Englischen übersetzt von Monika Baark ; illustriert von Kera Till. — Ditzingen : Reclam, 2021. — 586 Seiten : Illustrationen ; 25 cm. — ISBN 9783150113578

    Content: German translation by Monika Baark, illustrated by Kera Till.

  18. Little Women : vier Schwestern halten zusammen / Louisa May Alcott ; aus dem Englischen von Bettina Münch. — Neu übersetzte Ausgabe, 1. Auflage. — Zürich : Atrium Verlag, 2020. — 399 Seiten ; 22 cm. — ISBN 9783855356539

    Content: The first of two volumes of a new German translation by Bettina Münch, published after the 2019 film. Contains the first volume of the novel.

  19. Little Women : vier Schwestern finden ihren Weg / Louisa May Alcott ; aus dem Englischen von Bettina Münch. — Neu übersetzte Ausgabe, 1. Auflage. — Zürich : Atrium Verlag, 2021. — 431 Seiten ; 22 cm. — ISBN 9783855356577

    Content: The second volume of Bettina Münch's German translation, containing the second volume of the novel (Good Wives).

  20. Betty und ihre Schwestern / Louisa May Alcott ; aus dem Englischen übersetzt und bearbeitet von Inge M. Artl. — 1. Auflage. — Würzburg : Arena, 2014. — 233 Seiten ; 20 cm. — (Arena-Kinderbuch-Klassiker). — ISBN 9783401069739

    Content: German version for children, translated and adapted by Inge M. Artl. Beth is called Betty, as in older German editions.

  21. Marie Bierstedt liest Louisa May Alcott, Betty und ihre Schwestern / Regie: Kati Schaefer ; aus dem Amerikanischen übersetzt von Inge M. Artl. — Bearbeitete Fassung. — Bergisch Gladbach : Lübbe, 2009. — 4 CDs ; 12 cm. — (Lübbe audio - Bücher zum Hören). — ISBN 9783785738597

    Content: German audiobook on four CDs: the actress Marie Bierstedt reads a shortened version of Inge M. Artl's translation for children.
